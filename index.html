ASTBIO 201 · Bachelor of Science in AstronomyAstrobiology

This course develops the biological knowledge needed to study life in the universe. Term 1 introduces general biology, cell structure and function, genetics, evolution, taxonomy, microorganisms, botany, zoology, and ecology. Term 2 examines human anatomy and physiology, homeostasis, biophysics, and the physics of the human body. Term 3 focuses on space life science and astrobiology, including origins of life, planetary habitability, extremophiles, biosignatures, life-detection strategies, biological responses to space environments, closed ecosystems, and planetary protection.
