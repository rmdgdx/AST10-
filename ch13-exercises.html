IBiosignatures and Life-Detection Science Items 1–10

Interpret potential biosignatures and design a life-detection sequence that addresses controls, contamination, and ambiguity.

1.Oxygen alone is a weak exoplanet biosignature…Analyze

Oxygen alone is a weak exoplanet biosignature because it

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: A

Photochemical routes on water-rich planets, particularly around active M dwarfs, can produce substantial oxygen with no biology, which is why disequilibrium pairs are preferred.

2.The strongest argument for oxygen and methane…Evaluate

The strongest argument for oxygen and methane together as a biosignature is that

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: D

Coexistence of a reduced and an oxidised species at detectable levels requires a large ongoing source, which is difficult to sustain abiotically.

3.Enantiomeric excess in a returned sample would…Analyze

Enantiomeric excess in a returned sample would be a meaningful biosignature because abiotic chemistry generally produces

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: C

Without a chiral-selecting process, both forms are produced roughly equally, and any excess racemises over geological time unless maintained.

4.The main lesson of the ALH 84001…Analyze

The main lesson of the ALH 84001 debate for life-detection practice is that

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: B

Each individual line had an abiotic explanation. Robustness comes from lines that fail in different ways, not from the number of lines.

5.A procedural blank differs from an instrument…Analyze

A procedural blank differs from an instrument blank because it is

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: C

Only a full-chain blank captures contamination introduced by handling, containers, and reagents rather than by the detector alone.

6.In a staged detection designAnalyze

In a staged detection design, contextual habitability assessment comes first because it

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: B

Context governs both sampling strategy and the prior weight given to any anomaly, and it is comparatively cheap to establish.

7.Two instruments with correlated failure modes provide…Analyze

Two instruments with correlated failure modes provide weaker confirmation than two with independent failure modes because

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: A

Agreement is only evidence if the methods could have disagreed for independent reasons; shared systematics defeat that.

8.Reporting a detection on a confidence scale…Evaluate

Reporting a detection on a confidence scale rather than as a binary claim is preferred because it

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: C

A scale makes the state of the evidence legible and allows the claim to be revised as further requirements are met or fail.

9.Which sample setting would generally have the…Analyze

Which sample setting would generally have the highest preservation potential for organic biosignatures on Mars?

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: D

Ionising radiation degrades surface organics over millions of years; fine-grained, shielded deposits retain them far better.

10.A technosignature search differs from a biosignature…Analyze

A technosignature search differs from a biosignature search principally in that it

Reveal answer and explanation

Correct answer: B

Technosignatures are highly specific if found, but the search space is defined by assumptions about technology, and terrestrial radio interference is a persistent false-positive source.

IIApplied tasks Portfolio / notebook

Assessment tasks for the week: Life-detection case study; method design.

Task 1Astrobiology Case StudyCreate

Astrobiology Case Study: take a published or instructor-supplied biosignature claim and evaluate it. State the observation, the biological interpretation, at least two abiotic alternatives, the contamination controls reported, and the confidence level you would assign with justification.

Instructor note and grading focus

The grade rests on the abiotic alternatives and the controls section, not on the verdict reached.

Rubric: Case-study rubric; validity checklist.

Task 2Astrobiology Case StudyCreate

Design a four-stage life-detection sequence for a specific target environment. For each stage give the measurement, the instrument, the decision rule for proceeding, and the false-positive mode it is intended to exclude.

Instructor note and grading focus

Decision rules must be stated before results, which is the point of a staged design.

Rubric: Case-study rubric; validity checklist.
